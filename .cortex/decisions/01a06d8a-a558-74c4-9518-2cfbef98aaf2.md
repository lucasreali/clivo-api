`open-in-view` is false, so a `RecordTemplate` returned from a transactional service method arrives at the controller detached, and touching its lazy `sections` collection throws. Making the two nested `@OneToMany` collections EAGER is not an option either: two ordered `List` associations fetched together raise Hibernate's `MultipleBagFetchException`.

`RecordTemplateService` therefore returns `TemplateSnapshot`, built inside the transaction by `RecordTemplate.snapshot()`. The snapshot reuses the very same `TemplateContent` / `SectionContent` / `FieldContent` records that the write side accepts, so what a caller submits is exactly the shape it reads back, and the controller's `RecordTemplateView` maps from the snapshot rather than from entities.

A side benefit: `TemplateSection` and `TemplateField` expose `content()` instead of a getter per column, keeping the entities free of the accessor sprawl the project's standards warn against.
