`AuditableEntity` (phase 0.4) declares the four columns `created_at`, `created_by`, `updated_at`, `updated_by`. The physical `customer` table ships only three of them — there is no `created_by`. With `ddl-auto: validate` an entity extending `AuditableEntity` would fail startup.

`Customer` therefore extends `TenantScopedEntity` and carries `@EntityListeners(AuditingEntityListener.class)` with `@CreatedDate`, `@LastModifiedDate` and `@LastModifiedBy` on its own three fields. Spring Data still fills them from the same `AuditorAware`; only the mapped superclass is skipped.

Rejected: a migration adding `customer.created_by`. The physical schema is the project's given, and `updated_by` alone is what it chose to keep for the customer trail.

The same check applies to every phase-2 table: `practitioner`, `service` and `availability` carry no audit columns at all, so those entities extend `TenantScopedEntity` with none. `AuditableEntity` fits only tables that declare all four — today just the test fixture `sample_entity`.
