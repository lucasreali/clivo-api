6.9 needed a per-practitioner share and a way to close a period; the shipped schema had neither — `commission` carried `percentage` and `amount` but no settlement state, and `practitioner` carried no rate.

`V10__commission_settlement.sql` adds `status`/`closed_at` to `commission` and a new `commission_rate` table owned by the module (`PractitionerCommission`, unique per tenant and practitioner). Putting the rate on `core.practitioner` would have pushed a module concept into the core table and made a clinic without the module carry a meaningless column. The module references the practitioner through `core :: practitioner`; core learns nothing.

Accrual runs as a third `EncounterCompletionListener`, `@Order(200)` — after `InvoiceIssuer` (0) and `PackageSessionConsumer` (100) — so the commission is computed on the invoice's FINAL net amount: an encounter covered by a package or reduced by insurance earns proportionally less, with no special case in the code. A practitioner with no agreed rate, or a rate of zero, accrues nothing at all rather than a zero row.

Closing is a period operation on `CommissionStatement`; closing a period with nothing open is refused, which also makes a double close impossible.
