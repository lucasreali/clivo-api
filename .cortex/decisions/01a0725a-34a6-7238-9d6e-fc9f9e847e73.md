Phase 5 was built in the order 5.3, 5.1, 5.2 rather than the todo's numbering. Reason: 5.1 ("todo atendimento concluído gera cobrança") needs the extension point, and 5.3's own acceptance criterion — "o núcleo compila sem nenhuma implementação existir" — can only be demonstrated while no implementation exists. Building it first let `EncounterCompletionExtensionTest` prove exactly that.

Design points worth keeping:

- `EncounterService` announces to `List<EncounterCompletionListener>` inside the completion transaction, so a listener that refuses leaves the encounter open. `InvoiceIssuer` in `core/billing` is the first listener; `EncounterService` never mentions billing. Task 6.6 (session packages) plugs in the same way.
- `Invoice` is the aggregate root and owns its `Payment` list. `outstandingBalance()` is `netAmount` minus the sum of the non-refunded payments, computed by folding `payment.addTo(total)` rather than reading amounts out of each payment. `InvoiceStatus` is derived from the balance after every settle, refund and discount, never assigned by the service.
- `BillingService` reaches the encounter through the new `EncounterBook` in `core/encounter` (mirroring `AppointmentBook`), not through `EncounterService`. Injecting `EncounterService` would close a bean cycle, because that service already depends on the listener list that `InvoiceIssuer` belongs to.
- `payment.recorded_by` is `NOT NULL` with a foreign key to `app_user`, so `BillingService` resolves the recorder from `AuditorAware<Long>` and refuses with a clear business message when nobody is authenticated. `BillingFixture` therefore registers a real manager and binds it to the `SecurityContextHolder`; it is the first test fixture in the project that has to authenticate.
- Due date is the day the invoice is issued — pay at the desk. No `invoice_due_days` parameter was added; the seed has `late_interest_pct` and `late_fee_pct` but no due-days parameter, so this stays consistent with it. A grace period is a parameter to add later if wanted.
- `coverage` defaults to DIRECT and there is deliberately no mutator yet. The rule that decides SESSION_PACKAGE or INSURANCE coverage arrives with 6.6 and 6.7, and 6.7 already calls for a billing-adjustment extension point; building the setter now would guess at its shape.

Migrations: V5 puts the service on the encounter, V6 adds `invoice.discount_reason`, `invoice.coverage`, the discount-reason check and the unique constraint on `encounter_id`, V7 adds the payment refund columns and their check.
