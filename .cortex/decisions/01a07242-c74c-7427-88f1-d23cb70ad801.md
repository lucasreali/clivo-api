Task 4.4 asks the customer history to show different things depending on the `role_model` parameter: under SEGREGATED, reception sees date, practitioner and service while the clinical content stays with the practitioner; under SINGLE, everyone sees everything. The todo places the `RoleAccessPolicy` Strategy in task 6.5 (financial report), but there is no way to satisfy 4.4 without that decision existing somewhere, and writing an `if` on `role_model` now would be code 6.5 explicitly forbids and would have to be deleted.

So the Strategy was built here instead of at 6.5:

- `RoleAccessPolicy` and `ViewerRole` in `common/extension` (per the standing rule that pattern interfaces live there, never in `patterns`).
- `SegregatedRoleAccessPolicy` (`@Component("SEGREGATED")`) and `SingleRoleAccessPolicy` (`@Component("SINGLE")`) in `patterns/strategy`. Bean name equals the `role_model` value, exactly the mechanism 6.4 prescribes for `ExpiredBatchPolicy`.
- `RoleAccess` in `core/access` resolves the policy from `Map<String, RoleAccessPolicy>` keyed by the parameter value. No if, no switch, no enum. An unregistered role model is refused with a `BusinessException` naming it.

6.5 now only has to add the financial-report use of the same policy.

`ViewerRole` exists because `common` cannot import `core`, so the port cannot mention `Role`. `Role.asViewer()` converts. To keep the segregated policy from hardcoding a role name that could silently drift, `patterns` gained `core :: access` in its `allowedDependencies` and the policy derives its constant from `Role.PRACTITIONER.name()`; `core/access` got a `@NamedInterface("access")` package-info for that.

The history endpoint is `GET /api/encounters?customerId=`. Its view is `@JsonInclude(NON_NULL)`, so a hidden record is an absent `sheet` field, not `"sheet": null` — the same no-trace rule the inactive modules follow. An unauthenticated caller is treated as `RECEPTION`, the least privileged clinic role.
